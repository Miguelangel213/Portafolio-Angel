# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Freelance clients — individuals and businesses looking to hire a developer for web projects, Telegram bots, Roblox game development, AI automation, or marketing digital work. Spanish-speaking, primarily Latin American market.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that showcases Ángel Gomez's skills, projects, and availability to attract freelance work. Success means a visitor is impressed enough to reach out via email, WhatsApp, or social channels to hire Ángel for a project.

## Positioning

A young developer from Medellín who combines web development, game development (Roblox Luau), Telegram bot creation, AI integration, and marketing digital into a single creative offering — a breadth uncommon at this career stage.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from direct links, social media, or job platforms. They browse on both desktop and mobile. The site is a single-page portfolio with sections: hero, about, projects, interactive bot demo, and contact. Deployed on Netlify with a serverless function powering the chatbot (Groq API / Llama 3.1).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static HTML/CSS/JS — no build step, no framework
- Netlify hosting with serverless functions
- AI chatbot (POP'S BOT) as a live interactive demo, powered by Groq
- Four showcase projects: clothing brand e-commerce, creative landing page, Roblox game (Social Club), Telegram crypto bot (POP'S)
- 1 year of experience, 6 projects total
- Content entirely in Spanish

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **Ángel Gomez** — displayed as-is
- Avatar: "Character design.jpg" NFT-style character — used as favicon and about photo
- Color identity: dark background (#0a0a0a), neon green accent (#00ff88), orange secondary (#ff6b35)
- Bot identity: **POP'S BOT** — name and personality are intentional
- Tagline rotation: "Desarrollador Web", "Marketing Digital", "Roblox Luau", "Creatividad"
- Footer: "Hecho en Medellín 🇨🇴"

## Evidence on Hand

- Four live project links (Netlify-hosted sites, Roblox game, Telegram bot)
- Screenshots for each project (PNG files in repo root)
- NFT-style character avatar
- Working AI chatbot demo with real API integration
- No testimonials, case studies, or press. Future work must not fabricate these.

## Product Principles

1. **Show, don't tell** — live demos and real project links prove ability better than claims.
2. **Accessibility through personality** — the bot demo, animations, and conversational tone lower the barrier to reaching out.
3. **Breadth as strength** — the range of skills (web, games, bots, AI, marketing) is the differentiator, not a weakness to hide.
4. **Availability is the CTA** — every section should reinforce that Ángel is ready to take on new work now.
